## Contract and acceptance closure

1. `reelSequences` supports exact/weighted entries with stable local IDs and whole
   ordered arrays. Selection is per reel/logical round; initialization is separate,
   direct play has bounded implicit contexts, and built-in decorators retain choices
   through free continuations. Canonical artifact metadata, hashing, replay, JSON
   restoration, File/SQLite persistence, retries and rollback retain that identity.
2. One definition validator serves JSON/build, Studio save/validation and direct
   construction. Invalid collections, IDs, weights, duplicate arrays, unknown
   symbols, unequal variant lengths, reel counts and conflicting sources fail before
   draws/publication. Zero weights are retained but never selected. Studio keeps
   invalid drafts available and preserves optimistic source-hash checks.
3. Literal sequences keep their existing seeded stop/math behavior and gain no
   variant draw. Equal lengths are required within a weighted reel; circular windows
   may wrap, and different reels may differ in length. Generated/recompiled packages
   retain definitions. PAR/WASM preflight/execution and alternate generators reject
   unsupported weighted inputs. Public ESM/CJS interoperation is tested.
4. Configured weights, exact probability ratios, observed counts/frequencies and
   uncovered/zero-weight entries flow through run-local simulation deltas,
   snapshots/chunks/merges and all report formats/Studio. State retains configured
   counters plus active choices. Joint variant/stop enumeration merges grid mass in
   memory/disk, binds checkpoint identities and samples weighted events. The tiny
   1:3 fixture has exact RTP 1.0; seeded simulation tolerance is 0.06 absolute RTP.

Support and public examples are documented in `reels-and-sequences.md`,
`reel-strip-generation.md`, `game-packages.md`, `math-modeling.md`, `simulation.md`,
`weighted-outcome-library.md` and `wasm-compatibility-boundary.md`.
