## Support limits

Alternative collectors, triggers, payout aggregators, random or stake-dependent jackpot triggers, custom contributions, multi-tier award selection, shared/distributed progressive storage and custom entry handlers remain custom-only. A built-in wrapper alone does not certify their extraction or restoration. Authored accumulating pools are session-local; simulation workers each own independent pools, never one shared progressive. Fixed pools record contribution statistics without growing their fixed balance.

Metadata-only bet modes remain legacy declarations, not executable coverage. Current selection differs from original charged purchase attribution. Purchases require fresh explicit actions; simulation's fixed selector intentionally repeats purchases. Stepwise and composite delivery share logical-round accounting. Completed observations differ from incomplete tails; seeded replay differs from exact audit replay.

TS packages, Blueprint and PAR retain included semantics. WASM and paid-grid outcome-library/Stake projections must fail closed for unsupported feature histories. Build retains canonical routing, automatic supported intermediates, explicit save/apply and new/empty destinations. No top-level export command or packaging smoke is introduced.
