exact enumeration delegate execution. Injected sessions own the math and actual stake.

cross-store atomicity. Durable game state alone does not preserve in-memory wallet/retry stores or
Studio's registry. Support and extension guidance is maintained in `game-session.md`,
`serialization.md`, `game-packages.md` and `extension-points.md`.
