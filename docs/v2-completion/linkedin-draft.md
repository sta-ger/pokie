POKIE 2.0 is being prepared as ISC-licensed game math, a headless JavaScript/TypeScript runtime, CLI and local Studio. It is for people who want to author, inspect and reproduce slot mathematics, then integrate that work into their own systems.

A Blueprint can become an executable package or a supported artifact through one build workflow. Seeded simulation, replay, bounded optimization and dossier preparation help make assumptions and results reviewable. The local gallery/playground runs bounded examples with supported inputs and records seed, runtime and artifact provenance. Candidate access is through a controller-produced local archive or built checkout; pokie.fromstan.com is a future deployment target, not a verified live service.

Supported authored mechanics and composition/target limits are listed in the [support register](launch-mechanics-support.md) and maintained [authored-workflow](../../src/generated/AuthoredWorkflowSupportCatalog.ts) and [compatibility](../../src/generated/GameMechanicCompatibilityCatalog.ts) catalogs. Implementation coverage, historical execution and independent acceptance are different claims; candidate acceptance remains pending. This is not an all-features or production-ready claim.

Reference server adapters and local development tools are building blocks. Accounts, KYC, regulatory compliance, independent RNG certification and production RGS integration are outside POKIE. Transactional local storage and external-wallet outbox/reconciliation contracts do not provide distributed ACID; session persistence alone is not a durable wallet. Process-only Studio Play has no production settlement guarantee.

The default RNG uses Math.random. Seeded reproducibility, crypto-backed primitives, fairness proofs and certification dossier preparation serve different purposes. Simulation, structural validation, technical SDK compatibility and dossier generation do not establish regulatory certification.

ISC attribution and AS-IS terms apply; no managed hosting, commercial support or service-level commitments are offered.
