## Code-first extensions

Browser-safe TypeScript extensions can compose the shared session and calculator interfaces. Arbitrary Node packages and service collaborators cannot be lowered into canonical WASM. Follow the [complete extension source and runner](../examples/extension.ts) and [local reproduction guide](playground.md).
