## Examples you can inspect

Downloads are staged independently for this site. They do not rely on the npm package's `files` allowlist. Use the [sample Blueprint](../../examples/blueprints/sample-slot.blueprint.json) in the first journey and the [weighted Blueprint](../examples/weighted.blueprint.json) for whole-sequence reels. Complete [session](../examples/session.ts), [browser](../examples/browser.ts) and [optimizer](../examples/optimize.ts) sources accompany the relevant guides.

Maintained repository guides remain available as source, including [game packages](../../docs/game-packages.md), [simulation](../../docs/simulation.md) and [extension points](../../docs/extension-points.md). Campaign logs and historical receipts are not part of this site's content or search inventory.
